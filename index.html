Founded in 2024 as a spin-off of the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Dual Energy couples agriculture, engineering, and artificial intelligence into the predictive infrastructure for European agrivoltaics.

  1. 01 — Model

    Pre-permit feasibility

    A yield study bankable enough to earn the permit and unlock financing.

  2. 02 — Monitor

    Field sensors

    Microclimate, soil moisture, sub-canopy irradiance, and crop yield flow back into the engine — reality begins to match prediction, season by season.

  3. 03 — Optimize

    Continuous operation

    Real-time analytics, yield re-certification audits, and asset intelligence for the full regulated lifetime. Operational tuning each season and compliance dossiers.
